# 🌈 Speed Dating Experiment Dashboard

> *Who clicks with whom? Filter the floor, read the story, then test the chemistry yourself.*
> 

## ✨ The Story Behind the Data

What actually happens in a frantic, four-minute whirlwind of a speed dating event? Is love truly at first sight, or is it a complex equation dictated by shared interests, ambition, and first impressions? 

To find out, I turned to a classic dataset of experimental speed dating sessions[cite: 3]. Rather than letting thousands of interactions remain trapped in static tables, I built an interactive, vibrant dashboard that breaks down the science of human attraction[cite: 3]. From analyzing historical match patterns to predicting real-time chemistry, this app lets you step right onto the dating floor and explore the numbers behind Love and romance live ❤️❤️.

---

## 🚀 What’s Inside the Dashboard?

The application is structured into an immersive three-part journey:

*   **📊 Past — The Dating Floor (Exploratory Data Analysis):** Filter through thousands of dates by gender, age range, and demographics[cite: 3]. Uncover baseline match rates, demographic breakdowns, and the underlying distributions that shaped the event.
*   **🔍 Present — What Drives a Match?:** Dive deep into feature importance and behavioral metrics. Discover which traits (sincerity, intelligence, fun, shared interests, or physical attractiveness) actually tipped the scales toward a successful "Yes."
*   **🔮 Future — The Chemistry Predictor:** Put your own hypothetical date to the test! Adjust sliders for individual traits and instantly calculate the modeled match likelihood using trained machine learning classifiers.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

*   **Frontend & Interactivity:** Streamlit[cite: 3]
*   **Data Processing & Analysis:** Python, Pandas, NumPy
*   **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
*   **Predictive Modeling:** Scikit-Learn (Logistic Regression & Random Forest classifiers)
